This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the **TalkTalk Android app** and other applications (collectively, the "Apps") provided by Roman Kirillov.

## 1. Information We Collect

Our Apps are designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect any personal information for commercial purposes, advertising, or marketing. 

We may collect minimal technical information necessary for the Apps to function, such as:
- Device information (e.g., model, OS version).
- Log data related to App performance and stability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:
- Provide and maintain the functionality of the Apps.
- Improve and optimize the user experience.
- Respond to support requests.

We **do not** use your information for any commercial purposes or profiling.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except as strictly necessary to provide the service. 

Specifically, our Apps may use third-party APIs to provide core features:
- **Google Gemini API**: Some of our Apps use the Google Gemini API for processing data and generating responses. Data sent to this service is governed by Google's privacy policies.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third-party organizations.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

As we are based in the United Kingdom, we process your information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR). Our legal basis for processing is either:
- **Performance of a Contract**: To provide the services you have requested.
- **Legitimate Interests**: To improve our Apps and ensure they operate securely.

## 5. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights:
- **Access**: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Rectification**: You can ask us to correct inaccurate information.
- **Erasure**: You can ask us to delete your data.
- **Object/Restrict**: You can object to our processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Roman Kirillov via the blog at [https://blog.kirillov.cc/](https://blog.kirillov.cc/).
